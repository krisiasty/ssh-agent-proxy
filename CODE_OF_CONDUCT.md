# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project welcoming and harassment-free for
everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing technical disagreements directly.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus on what is best for users and the project.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's identifying or security-sensitive
  information.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, sustained disruption, and
other unprofessional conduct are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Report conduct concerns
privately to the repository owner using the contact options on their GitHub profile.
Do not open a public issue about another participant. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in repository discussions and in public spaces when someone
is representing the project.
